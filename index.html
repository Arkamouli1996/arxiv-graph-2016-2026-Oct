What counts as a paper?

One distinct base arXiv ID, dated by its first submission. Revisions do not add papers. Cross-listed field totals overlap; the overall curve counts each ID once. Primary-only counting provides a non-overlapping alternative.

What these graphs cannot tell us

PDF length is affected by formatting, references and appendices. The data do not identify AI-written papers or mathematical quality. COVID, changing field composition and existing trends must also be considered. The timeline now includes 2016–2018 as an earlier pre-COVID baseline.
